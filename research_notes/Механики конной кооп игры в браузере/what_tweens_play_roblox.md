# What 10–12-year-olds actually play (2025–2026): horse games and co-op-with-friends, Roblox as the main lens

Research date: 2 Nov 2026. All Roblox stats marked "API" were pulled live from the public Roblox Games API (`games.roblox.com/v1/games` + `/votes`) on 2 Nov 2026 ~16:40 UTC. Web search budget ran out mid-task; Reddit (old/new/JSON), Ofcom's own PDFs and Fandom HTML were blocked (403), so some kid-voice evidence comes from press interviews and parent-review sites rather than forums. Gaps are listed per section.

## KQ1. Which Roblox horse games are biggest in 2025–2026, and what is the gameplay loop?

### Takeaway
Two games dominate: **Wild Horse Islands** (~690M visits, ~5K concurrent on a Sunday afternoon, 92% likes) and **Horse Life** (~373M visits, ~2.7K concurrent, 96% likes, all-time peak 19K in Aug 2025). The legacy giants **Horse World** (650M visits but only ~500 online) and **Horse Valley** (463M, ~1.2K online) are coasting. The shared core loop is **tame/catch → train stats → breed for rare coats/species → sell or trade → race**, wrapped in weekly limited-time events; roleplay and customization carry the social layer. Actual show-jumping is marginal: the only games built around jumping/dressage/XC competitions are small alphas (**Horse Trials: Eventing**, 15M visits, ~480 online).

### Inferences
- The horse niche on Roblox is roughly 10–12K concurrent players across all titles on an ordinary weekend afternoon (sum of API "playing" counts), versus 200K–500K for single tween mega-hits — it is a niche with loyal, high-rating audiences (86–96% likes), not a mass genre.
- Visits-to-concurrency ratios (Horse World 650M visits / ~500 online) show the audience churns from old games to the newest polished one (Horse Life, 2023) — novelty + polish beats incumbency.
- Nothing in the top tier is built around jumping; the closest competitors to a show-jumping game are 5–50M-visit alphas with <500 concurrent players. A show-jumping game is entering an under-served sub-niche, not competing with Wild Horse Islands head-on.

## KQ2. How is jumping done in Roblox horse games, and what do players say about it?

### Takeaway
In the big two, "jumping" is a **trained stat** (Horse Life has a "Jump" stat; Wild Horse Islands trains "Jump" via Cross Country and "Hurry Scurry" arena activities), and competitions are **lap races through ring checkpoints with speed pads and sabotage items**, not judged show-jumping rounds. Dedicated show-jumping with faults/placings against other players exists only in small alpha games (Horse Trials: Eventing). I found no player commentary specifically praising or criticizing jumping feel.

### Cited Findings
- Wild Horse Islands "Training Island" (released 31 Mar 2023; free, no level gate) has "Indoor Arena, Outdoor Arena, Racetrack, Cross Country, Log and Tire Pulling, River Run, and Hill Work"; "Cross Country" trains "Stamina, Agility, Jump, and Strength" (23 receipts / 56 skill points); "Hurry Scurry" trains "Speed, Jump, and Agility"; other arena drills are "Single Stake, Pole Bending… Gait Control, Quadrangle, Figure 8 Stake, Speed Barrels". Skill Points unlock moves such as "Piaffe… Spanish Walk… Western Pleasure Gaits… Riding Double… Sliding Stop". — [WHI wiki: Training Island](https://wild-horse-islands.fandom.com/wiki/Training_Island); [WHI wiki: Skills](https://wild-horse-islands.fandom.com/wiki/Skills)
- Wild Horse Islands "Competition Hub" competitions are themed lap races (Pirates' Cove 2 laps, Lunar Base, Country Meadows 4 laps, Boomtown) with boosts ("Speed & Stamina Orb", "Swift Shield") and sabotages ("Lazy Spell… Spooky Ghost… Missle… Oil Slick… Rock Block"); placing higher earns Competition Tokens for an exclusive shop. — [WHI wiki: Competition Hub](https://wild-horse-islands.fandom.com/wiki/Competition_Hub)
- Horse Life "Horse Races": "fast-paced competitions where players navigate obstacles and aim for high placements within a 5-minute, three lap limit"; 3–15 players; a race notification spawns "once every 15 minutes… 70% chance"; "running through yellow ring checkpoints"; "green hoof boost pads… rainbow cubes… +25% speed"; "jumping over, water puddles will decrease the Players speed by -25%"; "Trails" (Jan 2025) are relaxed ring-guided rides with a speed cap. As of the 1 May 2026 update racing lives only in the "competitive realm". — [Horse Life wiki: Horse Races](https://horse-life.fandom.com/wiki/Horse_Races)
- Horse Trials: Eventing advertises real disciplines: "compete against other players in show jumping, dressage and cross country competitions". Hoof Beat lists show jumping, hunter jumping and cross-country. — [Roblox Games API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=2524014284)
- Non-Roblox reference for "proper" jumping input: in Rival Stars Horse Racing "you'll have to contend with jumps. These require you to nail your leaps with your horse… If you miss a jump, you'll take a penalty, which will affect your race time… you can redo tracks". — [Xbox Wire, 7 May 2026](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/05/07/five-tips-for-glory-in-rival-stars-horse-racing/)
- Developers on Roblox report horse movement/jumping is technically hard: one DevForum poster said "horse movement and features… such as walking on uneven terrain and jumping, are beyond what they can work out"; the Horse Life team said "Given the constraints of the Roblox physics engine, we had to be inventive in creating the horse rig and AI." — [DevForum thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/best-way-to-script-a-horse/525876); [Mane Quest Q&A 2024](https://themanequest.com/blog/2024/6/2/horse-life-on-roblox-the-new-fantasy-horse-breeding-game-is-now-available-developer-qampa3)

### Inferences
- Roblox's biggest horse games have abstracted jumping into a number; a timing-based jump over fences with visible faults would be novel relative to what tweens currently see on Roblox (and resembles Rival Stars on mobile, which Common Sense rates 12+).
- The racing designs tweens already know use ring checkpoints, boost pads, and item sabotage — familiar "Mario-Kart" grammar that a show-jumping course could borrow for readability.

### Gaps
- No Reddit/YouTube/forum quotes on how jumping feels in Horse Life or WHI (Reddit and Fandom HTML were blocked; no web-search budget left). The WHI wiki has no "Show Jumping" page at all, which itself suggests jumping is not a headline feature.

## KQ3. How do tweens play together in horse games (parties, private servers, roleplay, races, co-op tasks)?

### Takeaway
Together-play in horse games is mostly **parallel play in a shared world plus structured touchpoints**: trading hubs, co-breeding, timed public races every 15 minutes, private/friends-only islands and servers, and roleplay. Private servers are explicitly used to escape griefing and "grind in peace", and friends-only access is a first-class feature.

### Cited Findings
- Wild Horse Islands "Private Island": "a personal island that the player can edit… barn, pasture, riding area… able to be visited for free, but the player can restrict access to friends only or completely lock out all players"; customizable with purchasable décor and an optional indoor arena (Dec 2023). — [WHI wiki](https://wild-horse-islands.fandom.com/wiki/Private_Island)
- Wild Horse Islands "Breeding Hub" (Mar 2024) exists "dedicated toward breeding and co-breeding… helps players find other players to co-breed with much easier"; "Trading Hub" (Jan 2024) is a separate server for trading, level 15+. — [WHI wiki: Breeding Hub](https://wild-horse-islands.fandom.com/wiki/Breeding_Hub); [Trading Hub](https://wild-horse-islands.fandom.com/wiki/Trading_Hub)
- Wild Horse Islands trading culture: "Players do not trade on stats. They trade on coat and mane rarity, in vocabulary nobody explains to you" (IUH, IUC, MM, "VC = value check"); "There is no official value list… Anyone waving a 'confirmed value chart' to talk your horse down is running the oldest scam going." Island-unique hair is "1 in 1,000" wild horses. — [Earnaldo beginner guide, 2026](https://earnaldo.com/blog/wild-horse-islands-beginner-guide)
- Horse Life private servers: "Due to the high price of Horse Life's private servers, many players are unable to afford a quiet server to grind in"; the wiki hosts a page where players share server links and set "Allow Friends". Races are 3–15 players, spawned every 15 min. — [Horse Life wiki: Private / VIP Servers](https://horse-life.fandom.com/wiki/Private_/_VIP_Servers); [Horse Races](https://horse-life.fandom.com/wiki/Horse_Races)
- Horse Life trading happens in a dedicated "Trade World" (Jun 2024) with an auction-style stage. — [Horse Life wiki: Trading](https://horse-life.fandom.com/wiki/Trading)
- Store descriptions lean on friends: Horse Valley "solve mysteries with friends"; WHI "Trade, socialize, and roleplay with friends"; Hoof Beat "compete with your friends, create a private server"; Horse Area (alpha) ships "Share Your Horse (Beta)" and "Horse Emotes". — [Roblox Games API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=1024564064)
- Server sizes are small-to-mid: WHI 20, Horse Life 30, Horse Valley 16, Horse Riding Elite 9, Strideway 8, Horse Trials 10. — [Roblox Games API, 2 Nov 2026](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=2682938349)
- Parent-side evidence that private servers matter: for Steal a Brainrot, "If your kid plays, consider sticking to private servers. Playing with known friends strips out the random griefing and leaves behind a simple, harmless tycoon game." — [Screenwise](https://screenwiseapp.com/media/steal-a-brainrot-app)
- Kids' own complaints about Roblox social play (Wales, Jun 2024, n=1,284 aged 7–18): "29% of children who listed apps that made them feel unsafe or unhappy mentioned Roblox"; "children as young as 8 shared concerns about 'toxic and rude' users, 'aggressive behaviour', the ability to have conversations with strangers, being 'scammed'". — [Children's Commissioner for Wales](https://www.childcomwales.org.uk/?p=17447)
- Ofcom 2025: "Thirty-three percent of 8–9-year-olds who game online report playing with individuals they do not know personally." — [CAS summary of Ofcom 2025](https://www.computingatschool.org.uk/forum-news-blogs/2025/may/understanding-media-use-among-children-aged-3-11-key-insights-from-ofcom-s-2025-media-literacy-report/)

### Inferences
- "Co-op" in Roblox horse games is really co-presence + trading + occasional 3–15-player races; there is no shared-goal co-op task (e.g., a team course) in any of the top titles. A 2–4-player co-op show-jumping round with a shared score is unoccupied design space.
- Friends-only / private spaces are both a feature kids value and a monetization lever (Horse Life charges for private servers; kids complain). A browser game that is private-by-link by default removes a real pain point parents and kids name (griefing, strangers, scams).

### Gaps
- No first-person kid quotes about what they do with friends specifically in horse games (forums blocked). Evidence is from wikis, store copy and parent reviews.

## KQ4. Beyond horses, what co-op/party mechanics are tweens into in 2025–2026, and why?

### Takeaway
2025 was the year of **collect-and-flex loops with social pressure**: Grow a Garden (idle farm with a global shop restock every 5 min; 22.3M concurrent record), Steal a Brainrot (steal-from-neighbours tycoon; 25.4M record, 8-player servers), Dress to Impress (timed themed dress-up, peer-voted runway, duos), and co-op survival (99 Nights in the Forest, 14.2M peak; Dead Rails). Evergreen staples remain roleplay (Brookhaven, Adopt Me) and obbies (Tower of Hell). Mechanics kids respond to: limited-time scarcity, trading, "admin abuse" live events, short rounds with public ranking, emotes/avatar expression, and low-stakes "chill" loops that run while offline.

### Inferences
- Both 2025 mega-hits have since decayed hard (SAB 25.4M peak → ~98K online on 2 Nov 2026; GAG 22.3M → ~18K) — tweens chase the newest loop, but evergreen social/roleplay/trading games (Brookhaven, Adopt Me, MM2) hold 200–500K steadily. For a small game, the durable mechanics are the evergreen ones: short rounds, friends-only sessions, collecting/customizing, trading or gifting, and live events.

## KQ5. Do tweens care about realism in horse games, or about customization, collecting and social status?

### Takeaway
Evidence points to **customization, rare-coat collecting and trade-status** as the engine of the Roblox horse genre (store copy, trading vocabulary, 96%-rated fantasy breeding); **realism matters mainly to riders**, and the "realistic" Roblox games are tiny and sometimes paid. The horse-game critic community's consistent finding: real horse people notice movement accuracy, everyone else is "happy with much less".

### Cited Findings
- The highest-rated horse game (Horse Life, 96%) is explicitly fantasy — "magical species like the Kelpie, Fae Horse, and winged Peryton. Mutations like wings raise value"; Horse World is "Semi Fantasy"; Horse Valley is tagged "Fantasy". — [Earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/horse-life-vs-wild-horse-islands); [Roblox Games API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=485929611)
- WHI's economy is built on cosmetic rarity, not performance: "Players do not trade on stats. They trade on coat and mane rarity"; Island Unique Hair "cannot be bred, bought… or dyed on" — scarcity by design. — [Earnaldo guide](https://earnaldo.com/blog/wild-horse-islands-beginner-guide)
- Games marketed as "realistic" are small: Aurenvale (ex-Equestrial) "A realistic horse game… riding lessons, horseclubs" has 6M visits and ~90 online, and is paid access; the HorseCrazyGirls directory filters "Realistic / Roleplay / Eventing / Adventure / Fantasy / Casual" and lists 15 games. — [Roblox Games API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=5383076404); [HorseCrazyGirls](https://www.horsecrazygirls.com/best-roblox-horse-games.html)
- Horse-game critic Alice Ruppert (The Mane Quest): "Those who love horses know exactly how it has to look, but anyone who hasn't paid close attention to horse motion before will be happy with much less accuracy. Video games are a lot more careful about how humans look." Her reviews are "seriously being taken into account" by horse-game studios. — [Horse Canada, Aug 2022](https://horse-canada.com/magazine/business/horse-play-inside-world-gaming-horses/)
- Equestrian the Game (mobile, Kavalri, 2022): "a game for equestrians, by equestrians"; breeding "has always used realistic genetics as a basis, pleasing the sort of horse nerd who knows you have to pair a cremello and a bay to get a… [palomino]"; only two show disciplines: Jumping and Cross Country; training goals include "successfully jumping a number of obstacles". — [Mane Quest, Oct 2024](https://www.themanequest.com/blog/2024/10/16/weve-learned-a-lot-but-our-mission-to-make-great-horse-games-hasnt-changed-reflecting-on-equestrian-the-game-with-kavalri-games-molly-ericson); [TMQ database](https://horsegamedatabase.miraheze.org/wiki/Equestrian_the_Game)

- Avatar/self-expression stats: Gen Z change avatars to express creativity (47%), try a new identity (38%), stand out (38%); 73% want avatars to express a full range of emotions. — [Roblox Newsroom, Dec 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/12/roblox-replay-decoded-search-style)

### Inferences
- For 10–11-year-old riders specifically, the evidence suggests a split: they will spot wrong gaits/jumps (Ruppert's "know exactly how it has to look"), but what they *do* in-game is still customize, collect and show off. Design implication: get the canter/jump arc right (credibility for riders) but spend the content budget on coats, tack and a shareable podium.
- Hyper-realism as a selling point correlates with small audiences and paid access on Roblox; fantasy + customization correlates with the biggest, best-rated games.

### Gaps
- No direct quotes from kids who ride comparing Roblox horse games to real riding (Reddit r/Equestrian and r/StarStable blocked; YouTube comments not accessible).

## KQ6. Device habits and session lengths relevant to a mobile browser game

#### (d) Warnings: what tweens find boring or "babyish" (evidence-based, with caveats)
- **Being treated as the youngest cohort.** Adopt Me's base is "on average younger than the rest of Roblox"; Blox Fruits markets to a "13–24 core demographic"; 2/3 of Roblox DAUs are now 13+ and 13+ hours grew 107% YoY vs 67% for under-13. A 10–11-year-old is at the boundary — games read as "for little kids" lose them. — [Wikipedia Adopt Me](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adopt_Me!); [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/top-10-roblox-games-by-players-march-2026-data-breakdown); [Roblox Q3 2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509825000326/ex991-q32025shareholderl.htm)
- **Unexplained systems and grind walls.** WHI "hands you a lasso and a starter island, then explains almost none of its systems"; three separate currencies; level/token gates on islands. — [Earnaldo guide](https://earnaldo.com/blog/wild-horse-islands-beginner-guide)
- **Loss without agency / pay-to-win.** SAB: "losing in-game items… feels like personal property theft"; paid gear "grants higher movement speed… shield bypasses… pay-to-win frustration"; Horse Life in-app purchases "can provide an advantage". — [Screenwise](https://screenwiseapp.com/media/steal-a-brainrot-app)
- **Novelty-only loops die fast.** Meme games hold 8–12-minute sessions and "normalize rapidly once the novelty window closes"; SAB fell from 25.4M peak to ~98K online, GAG from 22.3M to ~18K by Nov 2026. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/top-10-roblox-games-by-players-march-2026-data-breakdown); [Roblox API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=7709344486,7436755782)
- **Stale or unmaintained games get abandoned** regardless of visits: Horse World (650M visits, ~500 online), Horse Valley Legacy and Maple Springs (0–42 online). — [Roblox API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=485929611,108689837)
- **Strangers, toxicity and scams** are the things kids themselves name as making Roblox "unsafe or unhappy" (29% named Roblox; 'toxic and rude', 'scammed'). — [Children's Commissioner Wales](https://www.childcomwales.org.uk/?p=17447)

### Inferences
- A 2–4-player browser show-jumping game aimed at riders aged 10–11 should (1) make the round the unit (≤5 min, visible faults/time, podium), (2) let friends play either side-by-side on the same course or as a relay/team score (shared-goal co-op is empty space in the horse niche), (3) ship a scarcity-driven cosmetic layer (coats, tack, ribbons) and a safe gifting mechanic instead of open trading, (4) run a weekly limited course/event, and (5) avoid anything that reads as a toddler pet-care sim.
- "Realism" should be spent on gait/jump animation credibility and course logic (what riders notice), not on simulation depth that correlates with tiny paid-access audiences.
